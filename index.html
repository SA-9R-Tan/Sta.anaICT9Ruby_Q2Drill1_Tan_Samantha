A beginner's Guide to Badminton


Badminton is a fast-paced racquet and net sport played indoors or outdoors where players score points by hitting a shuttlecock across a net so it lands inside the opponent's court boundaries.

basic rules in court
  • Underhand serve: You must serve below the waist.
  • Diagonal serve: The serve must go diagonally across the net.
  • One hit: Teams only get one touch to clear the net.
  • No net touches: You cannot touch the net with your body or racket.
Equipment
  • Racket: Lightweight frame used to hit the shuttlecock.
  • Shuttlecock: The feathered or nylon projectile volleyed over the net.
  • Net: The center mesh barrier dividing the court.
  • Shoes: Rubber-soled footwear designed for quick lateral movements.
the diference of 2v2 and 1v1 plays
  • Court Width: Singles uses the narrow inner lines, while doubles expands to the wide outer lines.
  • Service Area: Singles serves are long and narrow, whereas doubles serves are short and wide.
  • layer Movement: Singles requires solo endurance to cover the whole court, while doubles relies on rapid teamwork and switching zones.
  • Server Rotation: Singles servers switch sides based purely on their own score, while doubles partners only switch sides after winning a point on their own serve.
